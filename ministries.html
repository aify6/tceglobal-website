SERVICE DEPARTMENTS

Where Will You Serve?

Our service departments are the hands and feet of the Embassy. Each unit is a community of growth, friendship, and purpose-driven service.

Worship & Music
Our worship team leads the congregation into the presence of God through anointed praise and skillful musicianship. Vocalists, instrumentalists, and sound engineers are all welcome.
Media & Tech
Capturing, streaming, and amplifying the message of the Kingdom through photography, videography, live streaming, and graphic design.
Children's Ministry
Nurturing the next generation in the ways and wisdom of Christ through engaging, age-appropriate programs every Sunday.
Hospitality
Creating a warm, welcoming environment for every visitor and member — from ushering to event planning and guest care.
Creative Arts
Using dance, drama, and visual arts to tell the story of God's love in ways that transcend words.
Outreach
Taking the love of God beyond the walls of the church into our local and global communities through service and evangelism.
TCE School
Our school of ministry equips believers with deep biblical knowledge, practical ministry skills, and mentorship for kingdom impact.
Prayer Unit
Undergirding every department and initiative through dedicated intercession, early morning prayers, and prophetic intercession teams.
