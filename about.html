Our Mission

Raised for Such a Time as This

The Change Embassy (TCE) exists to raise and empower believers who will influence society through spiritual growth, kingdom leadership, and transformational living. We are a community anchored in the Word of God, fired by the Holy Spirit, and committed to making disciples who make disciples.

We believe that the church is not merely a building — it is a people. A people sent into every sphere of society: government, media, business, education, and the arts — to demonstrate the Kingdom of God in tangible, powerful ways.

Our History

From a Small Gathering to a Global Embassy

The Change Embassy was founded over fifteen years ago by Dr. Bukola Williams and Pst. Blessing Bukola Williams, beginning as a small fellowship of believers passionate about authentic encounters with God and meaningful impact in their community.

From a home meeting of twelve people, TCE has grown into a thriving congregation of thousands, with a school of ministry, specialized discipleship programs, and community outreach initiatives that touch thousands of lives across Abuja and beyond.

Today, the Embassy continues to expand — not only in membership, but in kingdom influence. Our members serve in government, healthcare, media, and education, carrying the culture of Heaven into every sphere they inhabit.
