FIRST TIME HERE?

We Can't Wait to Meet You

Visiting a new church can feel overwhelming — but at TCE, you'll feel at home from the moment you walk in.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Your First Sunday

From the moment you arrive, our hospitality team will be there to welcome you, help you find your seat, and make sure your kids are settled in safely. A typical Sunday service runs about 90 minutes and includes vibrant worship, a clear and relevant message, and time to connect with the community.

1

Arrive & Park

Free parking is available on-site. Arrive 10 minutes early and our greeters will meet you at the door.

2

Check In Kids

Head to our Children's Ministry check-in desk for a safe, fun, and age-appropriate Sunday experience for your little ones.

3

Worship & Word

Enjoy about 90 minutes of uplifting worship and a powerful, relevant message from the Word of God.

4

Connect

After service, grab a coffee and meet people. Our connect team will be around to answer any questions.
