Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit — and beyond.

YOUR FIRST VISIT

Visiting TCE

What time do services start?
Our main Sunday service starts at 8:00 AM. Wednesday Bible Study runs at 6:00 PM. We recommend arriving 10–15 minutes early so you can settle in comfortably and connect with our welcome team.
Where is the church located?
We meet at HOKF Gardens, 794 Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala Way, Utako, Abuja 900108, FCT. Free parking is available on-site. Our greeters will meet you at the entrance.
What should I wear?
Come as you are! We have no dress code. You'll see a mix of smart casual, traditional attire, and everything in between. What matters most is that you show up — not what you're wearing.
How long does the service last?
A typical Sunday service lasts approximately 90 minutes, including worship, announcements, and the message. Wednesday Bible Study runs about 60–75 minutes.
Do I need to bring a Bible?
Not at all — scriptures are displayed on screen during the message. But if you have one, feel free to bring it along. You can also follow along on Bible apps on your phone.
KIDS & FAMILIES

Children & Families

What programs are available for children?
We have dedicated, age-appropriate programmes for children from newborns through teenagers, running concurrently with the main Sunday service. All our children's workers are trained and vetted for your child's safety.
How does the children's check-in work?
On your first visit, head to the Children's Ministry check-in desk where our team will register your child, print a name tag, and walk you to the appropriate class. Pick-up at the end of service requires your matching pickup tag — all for your child's security.
GIVING & MEMBERSHIP

Giving & Getting Involved

How do I give to the church?
You can give during service via the offering envelope, through online bank transfer, or via our Paystack payment link. Full bank details are available on our Give page.
How do I become a member?
We run a New Members Class periodically for those who would like to formally join the TCE family. Speak to any of our connect team members after service, or reach out via our Contact page.
How do I join a service department?
We love people who want to serve! Visit our Ministries page to explore all our departments, then reach out to us and we'll connect you with the right team leader.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Reach out to us directly and we'll get back to you promptly.
