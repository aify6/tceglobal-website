TCE SCHOOL

School of the Teaching Ministry

Comprehensive biblical training designed to equip you for kingdom-level ministry and leadership.

WHY ENROLL

Train. Lead. Transform.

The TCE School of the Teaching Ministry is our flagship discipleship and training programme, designed for believers who are hungry to go deeper in the Word and sharper in their ministry gifts. Whether you're just beginning your journey in ministry or looking to add depth to years of experience, this school is for you.

Taught by experienced ministers, our curriculum covers systematic theology, homiletics, pastoral care, leadership principles, and practical ministry skills — all grounded in the African kingdom context.
